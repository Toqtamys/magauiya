What it does · 01

BOPASI is the platform flagship — the web build of Magauiya (ver. inner peace 1.1 C). The video is processed entirely on your computer: stage-drift compensation by phase correlation, scale calibration from the Makler grid (100 µm), head detection with morphological filters, Hungarian-algorithm tracking and debris rejection by classifier v1.1 (91.1% accuracy, AUC 0.974 on unseen videos). Output: WHO-5 and WHO-6 classes, kinematics and percentiles versus fertile men (Campbell et al., 2021).

  • ▸Concentration, M/mL, with a 95% CI across fields of view
  • ▸Motility by WHO-5 (PR/NP/IM) and WHO-6 (a/b/c/d)
  • ▸Kinematics: VCL, VSL, VAP, LIN, STR, WOB, ALH, BCF
  • ▸Percentiles versus fertile men (WHO 2021)
  • ▸Batch processing of several videos, CSV/JSON export

Input requirements

  • •MP4 (H.264) or MOV video recorded through the microscope camera in a Makler chamber (10 µm deep)
  • •×10–×20 objective with the Makler grid visible — it is used for scale calibration
  • •5–30 s per field of view, ideally 2–5 fields; at least 30 fps, 50–60 fps preferred
  • •Sample at 37 °C, neat (undiluted) or with a known dilution

How to use

  1. 1Press “Analyze” — BOPASI opens below.
  2. 2Drag one or more videos into the app window.
  3. 3Choose the chamber (Makler 10 µm) and optionally enter ejaculate volume to get total count.
  4. 4Wait for processing: tracks appear on the frames and a table below.
  5. 5Review the overlay and download the CSV/JSON report.

If the tool runs locally only

Needs: Python 3.9+ · FFmpeg (опц.)

git clone https://github.com/Toqtamys/magauiya
cd magauiya/python
pip install -r requirements.txt
python spermcasa.py video.mp4
  • ↳Offline build: download web/Magauiya.html and double-click it — no internet needed.
  • ↳On Windows you can drag a video onto run_analysis.bat.

Limitations and common mistakes

  • !At 30 fps VCL, ALH and BCF are underestimated; a/b classes use 1 s displacement and are more robust.
  • !Without 37 °C warming the class-a fraction is underestimated.
  • !Defocus, bubbles and heavy debris reduce accuracy; the Makler grid must be sharp.
  • !Not validated against manual counting — research tool.

What you get

  • →Concentration (M/mL) and total count if volume is given
  • →a/b/c/d and PR/NP/IM fractions, kinematics, percentiles
  • →Video with track overlay, CSV table, JSON
